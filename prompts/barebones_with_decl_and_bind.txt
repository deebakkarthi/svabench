- Copy over all the parameters from `foo`
- The port list should have all the symbols that you will reference.
- Declare all the ports as `input` 
- At the end `bind` `foo_assert` to `foo` and call it `foo_assert_instance`.

<output_format>

...
endmodule
bind foo foo_assert foo_assert_instance (.*);

module bar_assert(<symbols used>);
assert property (property_specification);
...
endmodule
bind bar bar_assert bar_assert_instance (.*);

Output nothing outside this format, not even backticks.
